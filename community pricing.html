Frequently Asked Questions
Is CommunityLink part of HospiceLink? +
CommunityLink and HospiceLink are separate products from We Market Plus LLC. They share the same secure infrastructure and login system, but serve different markets — CommunityLink is built for senior living (IL, AL, Memory Care) communities.
How long does setup take? +
Most communities are up and running in under an hour. We provide guided onboarding, data import support, and a live demo walkthrough for every new account.
Can I switch plans later? +
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.
Is there a per-building price or portfolio discount? +
Pricing is per facility. For portfolios of 3+ buildings, contact us at info@wemarketplus.com for enterprise pricing and multi-community configuration.
What's included in the 30-day money-back guarantee? +
If you're not satisfied within 30 days of your first subscription payment, we'll refund your full payment — no questions asked. Just email info@wemarketplus.com.
