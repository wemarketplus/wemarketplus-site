Privacy Policy

We Market Plus LLC · Last Updated: January 15, 2026 · Effective: January 15, 2026

1. Introduction

We Market Plus LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") operates HospiceLink CRM ("Service"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our Service. By using the Service, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. HIPAA Compliance

HospiceLink is designed for use by hospice agencies that may handle Protected Health Information (PHI). We operate as a Business Associate under HIPAA and execute Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with all covered entity customers. PHI is handled in accordance with the HIPAA Privacy Rule (45 CFR Part 164).

  • PHI is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Access to PHI is restricted to authorized users only via role-based access controls
  • All PHI access is logged in our immutable HIPAA Audit Log
  • We do not sell, share, or use PHI for advertising purposes

3. Information We Collect

Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, agency name, and billing information when you create an account.

Usage Data: Log data including IP addresses, browser type, pages visited, features used, and timestamps.

Customer Data: Information you enter into the CRM, which may include prospect information, referral source data, notes, and clinical data. You control this data and we process it only as directed by you.

Payment Information: Processed by Stripe. We store only the last 4 digits of payment cards. We never store full card numbers.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • To process transactions and send billing notifications
  • To send transactional emails (welcome, password reset, billing)
  • To provide customer support
  • To detect and prevent fraud and security incidents
  • To comply with legal obligations

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Service Providers: Stripe (payments), SendGrid (email), Render (hosting), all under data processing agreements
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or to protect the rights of our users
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, with notice to affected users

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including TLS 1.3 encryption, AES-256 data encryption at rest, role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication support, regular security audits, and automated breach detection. However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Upon account termination, we retain data for 7 years to comply with HIPAA requirements, then securely delete or anonymize it. You may request early deletion of non-PHI data by contacting us.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of non-PHI data
  • Object to processing
  • Data portability

9. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. You may disable cookies in your browser, but this may affect Service functionality.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe we have collected data from a minor, contact us immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via email or a prominent notice in the Service at least 30 days before the changes take effect.
